About Audio Converter

A local web application for converting YouTube and SoundCloud links to high-quality lossless audio files.

What is this tool?

This application lets you paste YouTube or SoundCloud links and download the audio in high-quality lossless formats like FLAC (Free Lossless Audio Codec) or ALAC (Apple Lossless Audio Codec).

Why lossless?

  • Perfect audio quality — no compression artifacts
  • Industry-standard formats supported by all audio players
  • Ideal for archival and professional use

How It Works

  1. Paste a YouTube or SoundCloud URL
  2. Select your preferred lossless format (FLAC or ALAC)
  3. The app downloads the audio using yt-dlp
  4. FFmpeg converts the audio to your chosen lossless format
  5. Your high-quality file is ready to use!

Technical Stack

Web Framework: Flask (Python)

Downloader: yt-dlp (supports YouTube and SoundCloud)

Audio Conversion: FFmpeg (converts to FLAC/ALAC/WAV/OGG)

Database: SQLite (tracks conversion history)

System Requirements

Prerequisites:
• Python 3.8+ installed
• FFmpeg must be available in your system PATH (for macOS: brew install ffmpeg)
• Internet connection for downloading audio files
