Tip: paste a YouTube playlist, channel, or SoundCloud set/artist link and every track lands in its own folder. Spotify or Apple Music playlist link? It will be imported: each song is found on YouTube and saved into one folder.
Controls how converted files are organized on disk.

Formats Supported

FLAC (Free Lossless Audio Codec)

Universal support, widely compatible with all audio players and devices. Ideal for archival quality.

ALAC (Apple Lossless Audio Codec)

Best for Apple ecosystem (iTunes, QuickTime). Lossless quality with better compression than FLAC.

WAV (Uncompressed PCM)

Truly lossless with no compression. Largest file size but perfect fidelity for archival and professional editing.

OGG Vorbis (High-Quality Lossy)

Excellent quality with much smaller file sizes than lossless formats. Great for listening on the go.

How It Works
  1. Paste a YouTube or SoundCloud link
  2. Select your preferred lossless format
  3. The app downloads the audio using yt-dlp (with progress)
  4. FFmpeg converts and embeds title, artist & cover art
  5. Click Download on the completed conversion!
System Requirements

This app requires FFmpeg to be installed on your system.

For macOS: brew install ffmpeg
For Windows: Download from https://ffmpeg.org
For Linux (Ubuntu): sudo apt install ffmpeg
