About video downloads
  • Video quality follows your cap in Settings › Format Settings (default 1080p).
  • MP4 and WebM play right in the app's theater mode; MKV downloads for external players.
  • Every video is verified after conversion — truncated or wrong files are retried automatically.
  • Beyond YouTube: most video pages work — Vimeo, Dailymotion, series and episode pages. Paste a link and hit Inspect to see if a site reads correctly.
  • Only download videos you have the right to save.
